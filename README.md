## 🧪 Current Research

### Methods for Handling Multiple Outcomes in Health Data

**MSc Dissertation | University of Galway**

Analysing data from the **SECOMBIT Phase II clinical trial** to investigate multiple clinical outcomes and disease progression.

**Methods include:**

* Kaplan-Meier Survival Analysis
* Cox Proportional Hazards Models
* Logistic Regression
* Win Ratio
* Net Benefit Analysis
* Multi-State Models
* Clinical outcome modelling in R

The project explores patient trajectories from:

**Stable Disease → Progression → Death**

and investigates approaches beyond conventional single-endpoint clinical trial analysis.
